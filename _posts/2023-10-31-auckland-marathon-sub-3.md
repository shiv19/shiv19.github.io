On 29 October 2023 I ran the Auckland Marathon in 2:59:56. Three hours, broken by four seconds.

It was not a clean race. I lost the sub-3 pace group after 25 km, fought cramping calves, and had to dig deep for the sprint finish. Twenty-nine weeks of training came down to the last few seconds.

I wrote up the full race two days later, with the pacing, nutrition, mistakes, and lessons.
